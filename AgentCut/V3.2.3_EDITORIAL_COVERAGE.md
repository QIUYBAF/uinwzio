# AgentCut 3.2.3 — Editorial Coverage / Anti-Template Pass

## Why this exists

EP07 Nether practical cutting showed that technically correct speaker tracking could still look like a slideshow when every static image used one long continuous reframe. 3.2.3 adds a restrained editorial-coverage layer without forcing more motion everywhere.

## New semantic operations

### `direct_dialogue_coverage(scene_id, intensity=..., reset_gap=..., max_shots=...)`

Builds a deterministic shot path from dialogue/caption beats and scene-level Cast staging. The grammar favors:

1. establish/group framing,
2. speaker close-up or medium close-up,
3. alternate/reaction coverage when another speaker enters,
4. a group reset across meaningful pauses,
5. subtle variation for repeated lines from the same speaker.

It does not split the canonical source asset or retime subtitles.

### `direct_attention_insert(...)`

Adds a short deterministic focus insert for an object/action/detail and merges safely with existing dialogue coverage. It is intentionally non-semantic about object identity: AI/vision supplies the point of interest; AgentCut supplies the safe camera grammar.

## Automatic direction

`compose_dialogue_scene(direction="auto")` may choose coverage for longer multi-speaker scenes. Short dialogue remains on continuous speaker tracking/stillness when that is cleaner.

## QA

- `LONG_SINGLE_COVERAGE`: long multi-line dialogue remains on one continuous reframe.
- `LONG_PRE_DIALOGUE_HOLD`: long action-only lead may need an insert/visual beat.
- `DENSE_SHOT_COVERAGE`: too many discrete cuts in a short scene.
- `TIGHT_COVERAGE`: crop may need headroom/framing review.

These are creative QA signals, not mandatory automatic rewrites.

## EP07 practical finding

On the diagnostic Nether timeline, four long-dialogue single-coverage warnings were reproducible before coverage. After semantic coverage, those warnings cleared while subtitle timing remained unchanged.
